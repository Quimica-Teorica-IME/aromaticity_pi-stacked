Pi-stacked (π-stacked) systems stabilize molecular structures, enable efficient charge transport and energy dissipation in functional materials, and provide a framework for investigating local aromaticity under spatial confinement. The previously developed Q_2-based aromaticity descriptors (ACS Omega 2025, 10, 14, 1415) are applied here to assess their performance in π-stacked polyfluorene systems comprising one to four layers (F1-F4) connected through σ-bonded methylene bridges. The descriptors are derived from the electric quadrupole second-rank tensor Q_2 of the distributed multipole analysis (DMA) expansion. The Q_2-based aromaticity descriptor trends were compared with the traditional indices NICS, PDI, FLU, and HOMA. The |Q_2 |(±1) and NICS(±1) indices are affected by interlayer coupling, thereby failing to describe aromaticity trends accurately. Conversely, |Q_2 |(0) and |Q_2 |_(ring atoms) reproduced aromatic and non-aromatic trends and showed that local aromaticity remains nearly unchanged from F1 to F4, consistent with the PDI, FLU, and HOMA. Combined statistical and Machine Learning analyses of basis-set effects (6-31G*, 6-31+G*, 6-311G(d,p), def2-TZVP) showed that |Q_2 |(0) and |Q_2 |_(ring atoms) are largely basis set independent for the analyzed systems, whereas |Q_2 |(±1) is more sensitive. Calculations including the GD3BJ dispersion correction show that, despite a substantial contraction of the interlayer distances, the main Q_2-based aromaticity descriptors remain largely unchanged. Overall, |Q_2 |(0) and |Q_2 |_(ring atoms) emerge as robust and transferable descriptors for local aromaticity within the π-stacked polyfluorene systems investigated here.
